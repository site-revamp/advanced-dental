# Advanced Dental — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Advanced Dental is a Singapore-based dental group operating 22 clinics across Central, East, North, Northeast, and West Singapore. The group provides general, family, specialist, and urgent dental care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on helping patients find nearby clinics, explore dental treatments, and access urgent care more efficiently through clearer navigation and a responsive, patient-focused layout.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Clinic Discovery

**Previous Issue:** The clinic finder appeared below patient reviews and displayed branches in a carousel, making location discovery less immediate.

**Implemented Fix:** Introduced a searchable 22-clinic finder in the opening section, allowing patients to filter by region, search by clinic name or MRT/LRT station, and navigate directly to individual clinic pages.

### 2. Simplified Treatment Navigation

**Previous Issue:** The homepage displayed 32 dental procedures in a horizontal carousel, leaving several common treatments outside the initial view.

**Implemented Fix:** Replaced the carousel approach with six visible treatment pathways featuring direct links. The responsive grid displays two columns on mobile and three on larger screens, with a separate link to the complete treatment catalogue.

### 3. More Accessible Urgent Dental Care

**Previous Issue:** The urgent booking shortcut was positioned further down the homepage under "Plan your visit," making urgent assistance harder to locate.

**Implemented Fix:** Added prominent "Urgent dental care" access in the hero section and fixed mobile navigation bar. Patients can initiate a direct call and receive guidance on selecting the appropriate treatment in the existing online booking form.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign improves the overall patient experience through:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Prioritised clinic discovery, treatment selection, and urgent care access.
- **Improved Navigation:** Reduced reliance on horizontal carousels for essential information.
- **Responsive Layout:** Organised treatment pathways into accessible grids across mobile and larger screens.
- **Stronger Accessibility of Key Actions:** Positioned important patient actions prominently throughout the experience.
- **Greater Visual Consistency:** Established a more structured presentation of services and navigation elements.

## Summary

The redesigned Advanced Dental homepage creates a more direct and organised patient journey by addressing three core usability issues: clinic discovery, treatment accessibility, and urgent care navigation.

The improvements prioritise practical functionality, responsive presentation, and easier access to essential dental services.
